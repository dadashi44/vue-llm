# Code of Conduct

vue-llm follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the code. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's report form](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or by contacting @dadashi44. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block people who break these rules.
